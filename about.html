The Approach

How Coach Hari Trains His Players

A systematic framework that eliminates guesswork and accelerates player development.

01

Tactical Game Sense

Pickleball is won in the transition zone and at the kitchen line. Hari teaches court positioning, patience in the dink rally, and when to speed up vs. reset the ball.

02

Biomechanics & Consistency

Eliminate wrist flicking, late contact, and unbalanced footwork. We establish muscle memory through structured repetition so your shots hold up under match pressure.

03

Patient, Empathetic Coaching

Whether training a junior player, a working professional, or a 60+ senior looking to stay active, Hari tailors the physical tempo and explanation style to each individual.
