Structured Player Progression

Pickleball Coaching Programs & Courses

Every player arrives with different court habits, athletic background, and personal goals. Coach Hari Mohan (PPA Tour Asia Medalist, 5.03 DUPR) delivers a progressive, metric-driven curriculum designed to build true match confidence.

Stage 01: Beginner Coaching ↓ Stage 02: Skills & Technique (Popular) ↓ Stage 03: Competitive Coaching ↓
Stage 01 • Introductory Level

Beginner Coaching: Foundation & Court Confidence

Learn the fundamentals, court positioning, basic strokes, serving and scoring in a supportive, zero-intimidation environment. Designed for new players, tennis/badminton switchers, and seniors starting fresh.

Book Beginner Session
01
Pillar 1 • Gameplay Structure

Rules, Scoring & Court Etiquette

Demystify pickleball’s unique rule set from day one so you never feel lost during open games or recreational social play.

  • ✓ The 2-Bounce Rule: Understanding why the return of serve must bounce before being played.
  • ✓ 3-Number Scoring System: Master server score, receiver score, and server number (1 or 2).
  • ✓ Service Footwork & Trajectory: Legal underhand contact, paddle head below wrist, and deep target box placement.
Outcome: Step onto any court in Singapore and play full matches with zero hesitation.
02
Pillar 2 • Stroke Mechanics

Grip, Forehand & Backhand Mechanics

Build clean muscle memory. We eliminate tennis wrist snaps, late contact points, and off-balance stances through structured repetition.

  • ✓ Continental Grip Mastery: One universal neutral grip that handles both forehand and backhand without awkward shifts.
  • ✓ Compact Backswing: Eliminating excessive racquet backswings to handle fast close-range balls cleanly.
  • ✓ Point of Contact: Always striking the ball out in front of your lead hip for controlled, repeatable depth.
Outcome: Consistent groundstrokes that clear the net cleanly without sailing out.
03
Pillar 3 • Soft Game & Safety

Kitchen (NVZ) Safety & Dink Control

Pickleball is won at the Non-Volley Zone. Learn how to stay balanced, avoid costly foot faults, and cultivate the patient "soft game."

  • ✓ NVZ Foot Fault Rules: Balance routines ensuring momentum never carries your feet across the kitchen line.
  • ✓ Push Dink Technique: Using your shoulder pendulum instead of flicking wrists to keep the ball low and unattackable.
  • ✓ Equipment Fully Provided: High-grade composite paddles and Dura Fast regulation balls provided on-court.
Outcome: Master the kitchen line so you can dictate rallies rather than defending frantically.
Stage 02 • Most Popular Curriculum

Skills & Technique: Elevate Your Rally IQ

Improve shot selection, consistency, footwork movement, placement, and tactical understanding of longer, fast-paced rallies. Built for 2.5–3.5 players looking to dominate social sessions and club ladders.

Improve Your Game
01
Pillar 1 • The Essential Transition

3rd Shot Drops & Reset Control

The single shot that separates recreational swingers from skilled pickleball players. Master landing soft unattackable balls from 40 feet out.

  • ✓ Arc & Apex Calibration: Calculating ball apex on your side of the net so it gently dies in the opposing kitchen.
  • ✓ Drop vs. Drive Selection: Reading the server's return depth to know instantly when to drop softly or drive with pace.
  • ✓ Emergency Mid-Court Resets: Cushioning fast, aggressive chest-high balls into the kitchen from the transition zone.
Outcome: Reliably neutralize hard-hitting opponents and safely work your way up to the kitchen line.
02
Pillar 2 • Net Warfare

Speed-Up Management & Hand Battles

When kitchen dinks accelerate into 60mph hand battles, panic loses points. Develop lighting reactions, tight paddle positions, and baiting tactics.

  • ✓ Ready Position & Paddle Height: Keeping your paddle at 10-or-11 o'clock with relaxed grip pressure for instant blocks.
  • ✓ Recognizing "Out" Balls: Discipline to let high, wild speed-ups sail harmlessly over the baseline.
  • ✓ Counter-Punching at the Chicken Wing: Targeting the opponent’s dominant shoulder and hip pocket to force weak pop-ups.
Outcome: Win the firefights at the kitchen line instead of flinching or popping the ball up.
03
Pillar 3 • Tactical Mobility

Transition Footwork & Error Reduction

70% of recreational unforced errors occur when players hit while running. Learn split-stepping, partner mirroring, and patient court recovery.

  • ✓ The Split-Step Trigger: Stopping and balancing your weight the millisecond your opponent strikes the ball.
  • ✓ Partner String Connection: Moving laterally as a unified wall to prevent massive gaps down the middle channel.
  • ✓ Unforced Error Audit: Cutting low-percentage slice shots and impatient attacks to immediately boost match win-rates.
Outcome: Transform frantic scrambling into smooth, effortless positioning that wears opponents out.
Stage 03 • Advanced / Tournament Preparation

Competitive Coaching: Tournament Tactics & Composure

Develop smarter tactics, pressure decision-making, doubles court stacking, and high-level tournament match awareness drawn directly from Coach Hari’s PPA Tour Asia medal podiums.

Train To Compete
01
Pillar 1 • Match Intelligence

Tournament Simulation & Composure

Tournaments are won in the mind. Learn to thrive in tie-breakers, close out match points, and disrupt the opponent's rhythm under pressure.

  • ✓ High-Pressure Point Simulations: Starting drills at 9-9 and game-point deficits to build unshakeable mental poise.
  • ✓ Tactical Timeout Management: Knowing exactly when to freeze an opponent's scoring run and recalibrate partner focus.
  • ✓ Wind & Outdoor Court Adaptations: Adjusting trajectory, power margins, and court depth for Singapore outdoor court humidity and breezes.
Outcome: Stop losing tight games in the final 2 points and build true tournament composure.
02
Pillar 2 • Doubles Systems

Stacking & Opponent Targeting

Maximize your partnership's offensive weapons. Implement full-court stacking, cross-court poaching, and isolating the weaker player.

  • ✓ Full & Half-Court Stacking: Keeping your dominant forehands in the middle regardless of which side serves or receives.
  • ✓ Poaching Signals & Net Switches: Clear non-verbal cues that let you steal high-percentage volleys out of thin air.
  • ✓ Target Isolation: Pinning the vulnerable opponent deep or in awkward backhand corners while neutralizing their stronger partner.
Outcome: Operate as a cohesive doubles unit that systematically dismantles opposing pairs.
03
Pillar 3 • Advanced Execution

Edge Control, Spin & Roll Resets

Incorporate modern pro mechanics: topspin roll volleys, biting slice returns, and disguised roll dinks that dip sharply over the net.

  • ✓ Topspin Roll Volley: Attacking balls below net height by rolling up the back of the ball into opposing feet.
  • ✓ Disguise Speed-Ups: Identical backswing preparation for both push dinks and sudden offensive wrist flicks.
  • ✓ Spin Decryption: Reading topspin, underspin, and side-spin coming off raw carbon fiber paddles to prevent mishits.
Outcome: Add pro-level spin and deceptive attack options that generate immediate weak returns.
